The competency-based framework

Most assessments test two levels. Siya measures all six.

Bloom's Taxonomy defines six levels of cognitive complexity, from basic recall to original creation. Siya maps every response automatically, giving leadership a cognitive-depth profile across every class, subject and term.

NEP 2020 mandates this shift. Traditional tests measure what students remember. Competency frameworks measure what they can do: analyse, evaluate, create. Siya makes it measurable.
